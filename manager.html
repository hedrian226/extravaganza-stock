How to manage stock

1. Add or editChoose the product, quantity, and unit: PCS, PACK, or CASE.
2. Adjust quicklyUse + or − for fast quantity changes.
3. Watch alertsPurchase Alert shows products that are low or out of stock.
4. Import safelyUse Import Excel / CSV when updating several products at once.
5. Delete carefullyDeleted products are removed immediately, with recovery if the save fails.

Tip: On mobile, use the search box first to find an item instead of scrolling through the whole list.
